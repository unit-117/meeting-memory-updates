More reliable automatic recording

  • Native meeting recording now continues when the meeting app replaces its call window, including while muted.
  • A verified call's existing microphone process can keep recording active when its window information disappears. Unrelated audio and output alone do not extend recording.
  • Completed recordings now show why recording stopped. This information is saved locally and retained through transcription.
  • Includes the menu-bar recording indicator introduced in version 0.3.5.

Existing recordings, settings, and the stable app signing identity are preserved. Regression checks passed; real-call validation of this fix remains required.
